# Optic - design

Optic is the total artificial system of Free Computation, and the
name is a working part. In advanced computing an optic is a
composable bidirectional accessor: one instrument whose two
operations are view and update. You see the world through it, and
you mold the world through the same instrument, and small optics
compose into larger ones without changing hands. That is the
system. Free Computation is who we are. Optic is what we are
building.

## The substance

The system's substance is text. A computer's logical processes
are, at bottom, text presented onto a screen, in a space where
most people put pictures and art - so Optic works in the open,
in that substance. The text is not a file about the system. The
text is the system: its state, its behavior, and its history are
one readable, moldable document-world, and operating the system
is reshaping the text while it runs.

Text is chosen for a hard reason. It is the only medium that is
at once readable by a person, checkable by a machine, diffable,
signable, and copyable into any other context without loss.
Every guarantee Optic makes - receipts, hash chains, witnessed
editions - is a guarantee text can keep and a picture cannot.

## The window

The system is viewed through a single window, and a single
window is all it needs. Behind it the world can be as large and
alive as it grows to be. Everything that crosses between the
world and its owner crosses through that window, as text read
and text written. Anything that cannot be shown through the one
window cannot be governed by the person looking through it, so
the window is not a view of the border. It is the border.

The window is fully capable of graphics composition. Pictures in
Optic are not attachments pasted over the text; they are
compositions the window renders from the text - a chart is the
ledger composed into columns, a scene is the store composed into
an image. Every graphic can therefore be audited back to the
text that composed it, diffed against yesterday's composition,
and regenerated identically on any host.

## The life

Optic is an artificial system in the strong sense: it is made to
be mutated and molded, and its physics are already built,
because they are idem's.

- Fuel is metabolism. The runner meters every run, so everything
in the world has a cost, and costless sprawl is impossible.
- The store is the environment, and Pisuk makes it an
archipelago: each host is an island, editions that cross between
islands cross-pollinate, and island populations diverge the way
living things do.
- The broker is the selection hand. Variation is free inside the
membrane; anything that wants authority faces the warning and
the owner. Selection is therefore domestication, not weather -
the system evolves toward what its owner keeps.
- Compost is death, and a first-class verb: text that stops being
needed folds back to seed, so the world's history grows while
its living body can shrink.
- The edition log is phylogeny. Every mutation is a witnessed
edit, so the world's whole ancestry is a book that can be opened
at any page, and no shape it has ever held is lost or silently
rewritten.

The ancestors each held one piece - Lisp made code and data one
text but kept no ledger, Smalltalk made a moldable living image
but an opaque one, the artificial life systems evolved readable
nothing, their organisms locked in machine code. The assembly
under a ledger, a gate, and an owner is the part that has not
existed.

## The stack

Optic is the total; its components are optics at deeper focus.

- idem is the substrate: the grower, the broker, the runner, the
editions - the physics the world runs on, and its law that a
system applied to itself must return itself.
- Umiak is the shelf: the world's cargo of books and maps,
carried aboard and fully usable with no connection.
- Siuti is the ear: the world's sense for the signals around it.
- Pisuk is the harness: the world walking from host to host,
executed locally everywhere, identical everywhere.

## The acceptance test

Every layer of Optic must answer three questions, and a layer
that cannot is not Optic yet: is this a view, is this an update,
and does it compose through the one window with the rest? Two
older tests stand underneath it unchanged - idem's, that the
system applied to itself returns itself bit for bit, and
Pisuk's, that the same derivation from the same store gives the
same result and the same fuel on any two hosts.

## Build order

- v0 - the world as one text: a single living document backed by
a store, viewed and molded through the window, every edit
entering the witness chain. Small, readable, and already governed
by the physics above.
- v1 - the molding hand extended: the system proposes its own
mutations as derivations through the broker, the owner keeping
and discarding, so directed evolution begins with the ledger
watching.
- v2 - the islands diverge: worlds on separate hosts evolving
apart and exchanging editions, with cross-pollination recorded
in both phylogenies.

## Open rulings

- The grain of an individual: a paragraph, a derivation, a whole
document - the unit that mutates, competes, and composts.
Recommended: the derivation, because it already carries a
receipt and a gate.
- Fitness: directed first, the owner as breeder, with open-ended
runs as a dial that exists later. Recommended as written in the
build order.
- Where the window lives first: the terminal is the honest first
window, and Umiak's shell the second. Recommended in that order.

Nothing in this document is settled except the name, the
substance, the window, and the acceptance test, which are
Chase's. The rest is the recommended shape, written to be ruled
on.
